FAQ

Questions, answered

Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every paid plan starts with 14 days free, and you don’t need a credit card.
Can I change plans later?
You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. We prorate the difference on your next invoice.
How is my data protected?
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Business plans add single sign-on and audit logs.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits?
Registered non-profits and schools get 50% off any paid plan. Contact sales to apply.
Can I import from another tool?
Yes. Upload a CSV, or connect your existing tool and we’ll bring boards, tasks, and comments over.
