    Which Halcyon is right for you?

    I want the quietest, quickest daily driver
    Aria. Four doors, up to 412 miles of range, and a glass roof. The Performance trim reaches 60 mph in 3.0 seconds.
    I need space for family and gear
    Orion seats seven and tows 7,500 pounds. Vega is the smaller choice with a flat floor and a hands-free liftgate.
    I’m not ready to go fully electric
    Atlas runs on electricity for about 42 miles and then switches to its V6. Regent keeps a twin-turbo V8 and a traditional fuel tank.
    I want something special
    Nova is a limited-production grand tourer with three motors and a carbon-fiber body. Only 1,200 are built each year.
