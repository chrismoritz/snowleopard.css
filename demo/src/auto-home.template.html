How long does charging take?
With a 350 kW charger, most models add about 200 miles in ten minutes and reach 80% in around 20. Overnight at home takes about eight hours.
Can I charge at any station?
Yes. Every electric Halcyon works with the Halcyon Charge network and with most public fast chargers through a built-in adapter.
What if I’m not ready for an electric car?
The Atlas plug-in hybrid runs on electricity for daily errands with a V6 for longer trips, and the Regent offers a twin-turbo V8 with no plug at all.
